The flow

Follows along as you click through the phone.

  1. 1Sign in as an employee
  2. 2Clock in on your lorry
  3. 3Daily vehicle check
  4. 4Review Active Loads & filters
  5. 5New Load — Site or Quarry
  6. 6Customer, addresses & vehicle
  7. 7Category, type & weight
  8. 8Signatures & docket image
  9. 9Submit — it lands in Active Loads
  10. 10Clock out at the end of the day

Clock in from the band on the dashboard. A defect marked on the vehicle check needs a note, and the office sees it straight away.

Tap the bookmark icon on any step to save a draft. Open an amber Draft row to carry on where you left off; a green Submitted row opens read only.
